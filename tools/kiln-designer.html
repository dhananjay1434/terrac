Why it clears — or doesn't

The kiln pivots on trunnion pins whose axis passes through the vessel centreline at the hinge height. Every point on the shell therefore sweeps a circle of radius r about that axis. The lowest a point can reach is hingeHeight − r, so the vessel clears the ground through a full tip if and only if the furthest point on the shell lies within the hinge height of the pivot:

clearance = hingeHeight − maxPivotRadius

Negative clearance means the rim strikes the ground mid-dump. The governing point is almost always the far top-rim edge — raising the stand, or moving the hinge down the wall, are the two levers that fix it.
