### C4. The forensic science moat (the "secret" — Slide 2/4/7 ammunition)
From our analysis of 777+ days of controlled lysimeter geochemistry (5 treatment arms) + our 145kt field dataset:
1. **Passivation:** basalt dissolution is front-loaded — Year-1 flush, then a silica/iron leached layer permanently halts dissolution. Year 2+ reverts to background. Observed dissolution cap **~24.7%** → realistic CDR is **0.074 tCO₂/t rock**, not the theoretical 0.3. **Anyone selling 5–10-year linear-dissolution credits defaults in Year 2.**
2. **The crusting trap:** dose–response is catastrophically non-linear. 400 t/ha exported **less calcium than control** (−1.37M µmol excess; observed 400/100 ratio **−58.6×** vs expected 4×). High doses + ultra-fine grinding clog pores (−27.5% hydraulic conductivity), waste 3–4× CAPEX. **Correct playbook: 15–25 t/ha coarse crusher dust (d50 ~38 µm).**
3. **Poor Soil Premium:** target degraded (Q1) soils — max yield gain (+47%), max weathering (acidic soils dissolve basalt faster), zero competition.
4. **Logistics kill zone:** transport is THE variable — profitable <20 km (~$4/t), dead at 100 km (~$20/t). Hence the 50-km-zone architecture around mine partnerships.
5. **White-box MRV:** registries (Puro.earth, Isometric) reject black-box ML. Our stack: **$30 EC sensors at 30 cm + Kohlrausch's law ([Ca+Mg] = a·EC + b) + ⁸⁷Sr/⁸⁶Sr isotope fingerprinting + hard mass-balance cap** = deterministic, auditable, **~$80/ha/yr vs ~$400 industry** (80% cheaper, continuous vs quarterly).
- IMPORTANT REFRAME: do NOT lead with "PINN/AI MRV" (our own research shows registries reject black boxes). If ML appears at all, it is "physics-constrained signal filtering" beneath a deterministic, auditable layer. Lead with **white-box, sensor-first, cryptographically evidenced.**

## STORYTELLING DIRECTIVES (how to write every slide)

1. **The arc is: Secret → Proof → Machine → Wedge → Team → Ask.** The secret (why others default) makes the audience lean in; the proof (7,356 batches, 145kt data) makes them believe; the machine (full stack) makes it durable; the wedge (dMRV licensing) makes it venture-scale; the team's age makes it unforgettable.
2. Every slide: **claim (≤10 words) → number → artifact.** If a slide has no artifact, find one in C9 or cut the claim.
3. Write like an ops report, not an ad. "342 accepted batches/day. Zero moisture-gate violations." beats any adjective.
4. Use the honesty weapons: "We only sell Year-1 ERW credits — because our own data says Year 2 doesn't come." Deliberate under-claiming is the most memorable trust signal in this market.
5. The demo is the climax. Script it: *most MRV demos show dashboards; ours shows a system refusing to lie.*
6. Never let the deck exceed 10 slides; push everything else to appendix (evidence links, data tables, lysimeter charts, unit-economics detail, security architecture one-pager).
7. All `[VERIFY]` items must be resolved or visually softened (e.g., "~" ranges) before the deck is shown externally.
